==== 1. Tsui et al. 2026, Evolution Letters: Seychelles warbler body mass (z-scored) ====
Published (paper, Results and Table 2): body mass declines with age; onset of decline 4.7 years (limits 1-8.5) from the
derivative of the age smooth; lifespan fitted as a linear term; age x lifespan interaction significant for body mass;
model choice among G, GS and S by AIC (delta < 4). Authors' code: model1.R (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21101620).
